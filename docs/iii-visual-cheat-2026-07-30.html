Total beginner guide for new Brand Ambassadors • No prior network-marketing knowledge required.

One line explanation for a 12-year-old: Build two team legs (Left + Right), make volume flow in both, turn that into cycles, and get paid.
What is the binary?

2-sided team structure

Every downline order goes into either your Left or Right side. Think of a seesaw: when one side fills enough, you can pay a cycle. You do not place people into specific people by “position.”

Cycle formula = 300 CV (weak) + 600 CV (strong) = 1 paid cycle in the same bonus week.

What counts as matching (the “leadership” story)

Your own cycles + your downline cycles

Matching is team-development pay in practice.

  • Your BA’s paid rank sets your matching %.
  • You match on team commission cycles for that bonus week.
  • If your team members build enough on their side and produce cycles, your team development is paying you.
  • This is why helping people in your team grow matters — more healthy legs means more volume flow and cycles.

Think of it as: “I build people, people build people, and when the whole tree cycles I participate through matching.”

L1 bonus upgrade

L1 can move from 20% to 30%

Requirement: in a 4-week rolling period you have 10+ active Customers/Brand Ambassadors and 1,000 CV from those people (including up to 200 personal CV).

Matching amount is calculated on $35 per cycle. Example if this upgrade applies: 30% × $35 = $10.50 per cycle.

Global Leadership Bonus pool (the second leadership layer)

Eligibility starts at 4 Star Director and 120 PV active. This pool uses shares from your own rank, enroller-tree ranks, and qualifying PGV.

  • Must have 5 active people and 300 PGV from them in 4 weeks (plus 120 PV active status).
  • 1 share for each rank level you personally qualify for (4, 5, 6, 7+).
  • Shares also come from your enroller-tree legs (one highest paid rank of 4+ per leg).
  • Additional shares from PGV: +1 share per 1,000 PGV (up to your paid Matching level).

This can be explained to beginners as: “bigger leaders in your tree can unlock extra checks when the team is doing serious consistent 4-week volume.”

The official plan calls this a 3% global bonus pool; share math is layered from rank and tree strength.

Starter 90-second teaching script

“You are a Brand Ambassador, not a salesperson only. You are a team builder. Put real people on both sides of your binary. A cycle is just when one side has 300 CV and the other has 600 CV in the same week. Every cycle is $35. If you are paid as 1 Star, you keep 20% of each cycle = $7. When your rank grows, your matching % grows up to 65% at 7 Star. Your leadership bonus is extra pay from team development — if your downline grows strong, you get paid through matching and leadership shares too. PIB happens when you sponsor a new BA: more CV on their pack means bigger PIB from that first order. So your job is simple: keep both sides alive, stay active, and help people grow.”

Common questions from new BAs

  • “Are cycles weekly or monthly?” Weekly: Monday-Sunday in the bonus period.
  • “What is the max weekly cycles?” Base 500, increased to 600 if 4 conditions are met.
  • “What is matching?” Your paid rank % of cycle value and leadership structure of the tree.
  • “What is PIB?” A one-time enrollment bonus on a sponsored BA’s first pack CV using 25% / 28% / 33% tiers.
  • “Where does the leadership story come in?” Matching + Global Leadership pool are exactly your team-development rewards when your tree runs correctly.

Glossary

Binary
Two-side commission structure with left and right legs.
CV
Commissionable Volume used directly in cycle and PIB calculations.
PV
Personal Volume from personal purchases, used for active rules.
PGV
Personal Group Volume from your team and sponsorship lines.
Cycle
One payout event at 300/600 (LLV/GLV) in one bonus week.
LLV / GLV
Lesser Leg Volume and Greater Leg Volume at cycle time.
PIB
Product Introduction Bonus paid when a new BA enrolls with a pack.
Matching
Bonus percentage of cycle value paid by your paid-as rank; includes team leadership structure.
Carry Forward
Rule that applies when one leg exceeds large volume limits (official policy and example says 10+ million CV note).
Qualified BA
Has one enrollment on each binary side.
Paid-as Rank
The rank the system pays you at for a specific bonus period.
Highest Achieved Rank
Top rank reached historically before any rollbacks.
