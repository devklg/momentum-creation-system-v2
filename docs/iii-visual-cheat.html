Fast Way to Explain It

Core Rule You make money from customer sales + team cycles. A cycle is paid when the two sides of your binary are balanced enough.

Cycle formula: 300 CV on weaker side + 600 CV on stronger side = 1 cycle
Payout per cycle: $35

4-Bucket Earnings

  • Customer Commission: 25% on customer net purchases (no shipping/tax)
  • Cycle Commission: $35 per cycle (weekly)
  • Matching: % by paid rank, from 20% to 65% combined levels
  • Rank Rewards: one-time cash at rank milestones
  • PIB: 25/28/33% depending on pack CV

Quick Teach Script (copy/paste for your teams)

“Imagine your business like a two-sided seesaw. If both sides have product movement, you get paid for customer sales and then each cycle your sides create (300/600 CV). Cycles pay $35 each, and as you rank up, you earn matching percentages from more team levels plus rank rewards. The faster your team becomes active and balanced, the faster your income grows.”
Compliance notes: Cycle rules, matching %, and rank progression are tied to active status and detailed time windows. This cheat sheet is a training starter, not legal/compliance language. Use the official plan and current policy notes for final compensation promises.
