## 1) The idea in one sentence

Think like a seesaw with two sides (Left and Right).

- Every order from your downline goes into one side.
- The team gets paid by cycles.
- A paid cycle starts when the weaker side reaches **300 CV** and the stronger side reaches **600 CV** in the same bonus week.
- Weekly cycle value baseline is **$35**.

Bonus period:

- starts Monday 12:00 AM MT
- ends Sunday 11:59 PM MT

So cycles are **weekly**, not monthly.

## 2) Easy definitions (12-year-old language)

- **Binary**: two sides: Left and Right.
- **CV**: Commissionable Volume used for cycle + PIB math.
- **PV**: Personal Volume from your own and customer orders.
- **PGV**: Personal Group Volume from your team.
- **LLV / GLV**: Lesser Leg Volume / Greater Leg Volume.
- **Qualified BA**: Enrolled + one sponsor on each side.
- **Paid-as rank**: The rank you are paid at for a bonus week.
- **Active**: PV status rule used in rank + payout logic.
- **Matching**: Your paid rank gives a percentage of cycle value (including leadership structure and tree context).
- **PIB**: Product Introduction Bonus paid from a sponsored BA’s first eligible pack.

## 3) Weekly cycles, maximums, and timing

What matters for new BAs:

- **Base cap**: 500 cycles/week.
- **Qualified cap**: 600 cycles/week if all of this happens:
  1) Paid as 3 Star or above.
  2) Have 3 legs of 3 Star Elite in the prior 4 rolling weeks.
  3) Reached 500 cycles in a prior week.
  4) Have 10+ active people and 1,000 CV in 4 weeks (including up to 200 personal CV).

Cycle payout examples:

- 500 cycles → $17,500 cycle pool.
- 600 cycles → $21,000 cycle pool.

## 4) Matching = leadership pay

This is the part that unlocks your excitement: when people on your team grow and cycle, you get paid in addition to your own work.

This is not “placement”; it is rank-based team leadership reward on qualifying cycle commission flow.

### Matching table

| Paid-as Rank | Match % | $ on 1 cycle ($35) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 Star | 20% | $7.00 |
| 2 Star | 35% | $12.25 |
| 3 Star | 45% | $15.75 |
| 4 Star Director | 50% | $17.50 |
| 5 Star Director | 55% | $19.25 |
| 6 Star Director | 60% | $21.00 |
| 7 Star Director | 65% | $22.75 |

### Multi-level example (same team flow, different paid-as levels)

If you have 1 cycle in the week:

- 1 Star earns $7.00
- 2 Star earns $12.25
- 3 Star earns $15.75
- 4 Star earns $17.50
- 5 Star earns $19.25
- 6 Star earns $21.00
- 7 Star earns $22.75

So matching is **one paid-as level only** each payout week — the rank you are paid as.  
Global Leadership is a separate bonus layer and is covered in Section 8 (do not add it to this table).

For several cycles in a week, multiply by cycles (for example, 5 cycles at 5 Star = $96.25).

### L1 upgrade

L1 can move from 20% to **30%** if in a 4-week rolling period:

- 10+ active personally enrolled customers/Brand Ambassadors
- 1,000 CV from those people (including personal orders up to 200 CV).

If L1 is upgraded, $35 × 30% = $10.50 per cycle.

## 5) PIB from packages (capture-based example)

PIB formula:

- 20–199 CV = 25%
- 200–299 CV = 28%
- 300+ CV = 33%

Using captured GLP pack CVs:

| Pack | CV | PIB % | PIB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GLP THREE 4 Pack | 130 | 25% | $32.50 |
| GLP THREE 8 Pack | 250 | 28% | $70.00 |
| GLP THREE 12 Pack | 380 | 33% | $125.40 |
| GLP THREE 16 Pack | 500 | 33% | $165.00 |

Use these as teaching examples with clear source notes and update as catalog is verified.

## 6) $60 BA examples

If one BA contributes about 60 CV:

- weak side: 5 people × 60 = 300 CV  
- strong side: 10 people × 60 = 600 CV  
- total = 15 people for one cycle.

Cycle value remains $35 then rank match applies.

## 7) Rank ladder (from starter to top)

Values below are sourced from the current plan chart.

### Lower-to-mid ranks

- Brand Ambassador: 100 PV in last 52 weeks.
- Qualified BA: 1 Left + 1 Right.
- 1 Star: 800 PGV, min 300 each side (52 weeks).
- 2 Star: 1,200 PGV, min 400 each side.
- 3 Star: 3,600 PGV, min 900 each side.
- 3 Star Pro: 50 cycles in 4 weeks.
- 3 Star Elite: 100 cycles in 4 weeks.
- 4 Star Director: active 120 PV + 200 cycles in 4 weeks + 2 x 3 Star legs (one per enroller tree leg, 52 weeks).
- 5 Star Director: active 120 PV + 500 cycles in 4 weeks + 4 x 3 Star legs.
- 6 Star Director: active 120 PV + 1,000 cycles in 4 weeks + 6 x 3 Star legs.
- 7 Star Director: active 120 PV + 1,500 cycles in 4 weeks + 2 x 6 Star legs (4-week period).

### Diamond ranks

- Gold Diamond: active 120 PV + 4,000,000 PGV in 4 weeks + 4 x 6 Star legs.
- Crown Diamond: active 120 PV + 6,000,000 PGV in 4 weeks + 6 x 6 Star legs.
- Royal Crown Diamond: active 120 PV + 8,000,000 PGV in 4 weeks + 8 x 6 Star legs.

Approximate $60-only person counts:

- 1 Star: 14
- 2 Star: 20
- 3 Star: 60
- Gold Diamond: 66,667
- Crown Diamond: 100,000
- Royal Crown Diamond: 133,334

These are training scale estimates; actual paid rank uses period windows and active/volume rules.

## 8) Global Leadership Bonus (advanced, leadership team bonus layer)

This is the “second layer” beyond cycle matching.

Requirements (from plan):

- Paid at 4 Star Director or above.
- Active at 120 PV.
- 5 active Customers/BAs and 300 CV from them in a 4-week period.

Pool shares can stack:

- By paid rank (4, 5, 6, 7+ each add share levels as cumulative in the plan logic).
- By enroller-tree rank per leg.
- By PGV bands: +1 share per 1,000 PGV up to paid Matching level.

## 9) Rank advancement rewards (overview)

- Rank-up rewards are one-time and listed in the plan chart (cash, token, certificate variants depending on rank).
- This handout teaches mechanics first; keep reward details as a separate section when discussing milestones.

## 10) Glossary

- **Cycle**: cycle trigger at 300/600 CV in one bonus week.
- **Carry forward**: special balancing rule at very high lesser-leg volume events.
- **Enroller tree**: where your sponsorship chain is traced for rank and share logic.
- **Tree leg**: one branch on your downline leadership structure.
- **Bonus period**: one week (Mon-Sun MT).
- **Active status**: PV-based status used in many rank and payout thresholds.

## 11) Script to read as training coach

“This is built for team growth, not perfection:
build both sides, keep customers and team active, and never stop teaching people to place on both legs.
When weak side has 300 and strong side has 600 CV in the same week, your team hits a cycle.
Each cycle is $35, and your rank decides your matching.
The bigger your team leadership, the bigger your matching upside when those cycles repeat.
PIB is your one-time bonus when you sponsor the next BA, based on their pack CV.”
