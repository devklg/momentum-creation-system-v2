# III International Compensation Plan
## New Member Starter Script (12-Year-Old Friendly)

**Built from 2602.V12.1.NAM-ENG (July 2026 plan extract)**

---

### Quick Opening (read this first)

“Welcome! Think of your business like a two-legged ladder. Every time you and your team buy product, you earn. Think of it like points from your own sales and bonus points from building both sides of your team fairly.”

### The Simple Idea

**You earn in 4 buckets:**

1. **Customer commissions (daily):** 25% of net customer sales.
2. **Cycle commissions (weekly):** $35 for each completed cycle.
3. **Matching bonuses:** Extra bonus from team cycles as you rank up.
4. **Rank rewards:** One-time pay/bonus at each rank.

---

## Part 1: The Terms in One Line

- **PV** = your own activity points from your personal + your customer orders.
- **CV** = points in every order (commissionable volume).
- **PGV** = team order points from your sponsorship lines + your customers.
- **Binary** = left and right team “sides.”
- **LLV / GLV** = lower-volume side / higher-volume side.
- **Cycle** = when both sides are balanced enough in the period.
- **Active** = 60 PV in 28 days (many bonuses require this).
- **Spillover** = people you didn’t place may still help your team volume.

---

## Part 2: What is a Cycle?

You need **300 CV on the weaker side and 600 CV on the stronger side** to make one cycle. Each cycle pays **$35**.

Think of it like this: if your left has 300 and right has 600, that’s 1 cycle and both sides get paid down accordingly by the system.

- **Base limit:** 500 cycles/week.
- **Possible boost:** 600 cycles/week if you hit all 4 higher-rank activity conditions in the plan.

“Your pay comes from customers and your team. Every order has points (CV/PV), and your team builds on two sides. If both sides keep growing, the plan makes **cycles** and pays $35 each.
